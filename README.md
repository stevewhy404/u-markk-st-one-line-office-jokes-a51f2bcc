Entry for the make.fun bounty "One-Line Office Jokes".

`main.py` writes a single office-life joke line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I emailed IT a photo of the jammed printer — it printed the photo, then jammed on that too.

The line is 91 characters, one line, and turns on a real office detail (the printer that jams at the worst moment) with the laugh landing in the last few words.
